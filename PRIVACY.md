Privacy Policy for LShot
Last updated: September 24, 2026
LShot is a macOS screenshot and annotation app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information LShot handles when you use the app.
Information collection
LShot does not collect, track, or transmit personal information. The app does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services.
Screenshots and permissions
LShot captures screen content only when you start a capture. Screen recording access is controlled by macOS and can be managed in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording.
Screenshots are processed on your Mac. LShot does not upload or send them to us or to third parties. You choose whether to copy a screenshot to the clipboard or save it as a file.
Settings and local data
LShot stores your app preferences locally on your Mac, such as your keyboard shortcut and save options. These settings are not transmitted to us.
Third-party services
LShot does not include third-party analytics or advertising services.
Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated if the app’s privacy practices change. The latest version will be available at the policy URL listed on the LShot App Store page.
